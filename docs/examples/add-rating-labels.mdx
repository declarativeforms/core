---
title: "Add rating labels"
---

If you have not created a form before, start with [Create a form](/getting-started/create-a-form).

```yaml form.yaml
version: 1
title: "Support feedback"
description: "A simple form with labels at both ends of a rating scale."

sections:
  - id: section_1
    title: "Feedback"
    fields:
      - id: satisfaction
        type: rating
        label: "How was your support experience?"
        min_label: "Poor"
        max_label: "Excellent"
        validators:
          - required

    next: done
```
