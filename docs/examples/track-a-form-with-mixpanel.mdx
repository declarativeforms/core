---
title: "Track a form with Mixpanel"
---

If you have not created a form before, start with [Create a form](/getting-started/create-a-form).

```yaml form.yaml
version: 1
title: "Lead capture"
description: "A simple form that sends form events to Mixpanel."

measurements:
  # Use your Mixpanel project token.
  mixpanel: "00000000000000000000000000000000"

sections:
  - id: section_1
    title: "Your details"
    fields:
      - id: email
        type: email
        label: "Email address"
        validators:
          - required

    next: done
```
