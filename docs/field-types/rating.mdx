---
title: "Rating"
---

If you have not created a form before, start with [Create a form](/getting-started/create-a-form).

```yaml form.yaml
version: 1
title: "Example form"
description: "A comprehensive rating example."

sections:
  - id: section_1
    title: "Section 1"
    fields:
      - id: question_1
        type: rating
        label: "Question 1"
        min_label: "Low"
        max_label: "High"
        validators:
          - required
          - type: min
            value: 1
            message: "Use a rating of at least 1."
          - type: max
            value: 5
            message: "Use a rating of no more than 5."

    next: done
```
